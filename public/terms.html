1. Who we are

Ekoyini ("we", "us") is operated by Ekoyini Holdings, 7 Torquay Street, Summerstrand, Gqeberha, 6001. Contact: support@ekoyini.co.za or WhatsApp 064 004 5465. Ekoyini is an ordering and delivery platform. Alcohol sold through Ekoyini is sold and supplied by our licensed partner taverns and liquor outlets, who hold the relevant liquor licences under the Liquor Act 59 of 2003 and provincial liquor legislation.

2. Age restriction (18+)

  • You must be 18 years or older to create an account, place an order or receive alcohol.
  • Our driver will ask for a valid ID (SA ID, driver's licence or passport) on delivery. If the recipient cannot prove they are 18+, or appears intoxicated, the order will not be handed over.
  • Refused deliveries for these reasons may be charged the delivery fee and are refunded less that fee.

3. Orders and pricing

  • All prices are in South African Rand and include VAT where applicable.
  • A delivery fee based on distance applies to every order; there is no free delivery. A service fee of R50 to R110, rising with the order value, is shown as its own line at checkout.
  • Prices, promotions and stock may change. The total shown at checkout is the amount you pay. If an item becomes unavailable we will contact you and refund or replace it.
  • We may refuse or cancel orders that appear fraudulent, abusive or that break the law.

4. Payment

We accept card payments (processed by Yoco), EFT and, for eligible customers, cash on delivery (subject to order limits). Card details are handled by our payment provider and are never stored by us. EFT orders are dispatched once payment reflects.

5. Delivery and trading hours

  • Every order is supplied by a licensed partner tavern, and is delivered only within that tavern's licensed trading hours under the Eastern Cape liquor rules, including any restrictions on Sundays and public holidays.
  • An order placed outside those hours is held and delivered when the partner tavern next opens. If you don't want to wait, ask us to cancel it and we refund you in full.
  • Delivery times are estimates. Please give an accurate address, a landmark and a reachable phone number.
  • Alcohol will not be delivered to schools, places of worship or other locations where delivery is prohibited.

6. Cancellations and refunds

  • You can cancel an order from your account until it has been dispatched. Once a driver is on the way, cancellation is no longer possible.
  • Refunds for cancelled paid orders are made to the original payment method within 7 business days (your bank may take a little longer to show it).
  • Damaged, missing or incorrect items must be reported within 24 hours of delivery to support@ekoyini.co.za or WhatsApp 064 004 5465. Your rights under the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 are not affected.

7. Responsible drinking

Please drink responsibly. Do not drink and drive. Not for sale to persons under the age of 18.
