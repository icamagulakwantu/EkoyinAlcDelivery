Driver rules and liability

Ekoyini Holdings (Pty) Ltd. Version 1.1, 23 Nov 2026. By taking a job in the driver app you agree to these rules.

1. The order is in your care

From the moment you confirm pickup (collection PIN) until the customer's delivery PIN is accepted, the order is in your care. Keep it with you or locked and in sight at all times.

2. Be vigilant, always

  • Check the order against the packing list before you leave the outlet: items, count, sealed bottles. Report anything wrong to dispatch before you ride off.
  • Go straight to the customer. No personal stops, no leaving the order unattended, no lending it to anyone.
  • Carry it so it cannot fall, tip or get crushed. Drive and ride carefully, and follow the traffic rules.
  • Keep your phone and location on so dispatch and the customer can follow you.

3. You are liable for breakage and loss in transit

You are fully liable for breakage, spillage, theft or any other loss of the items while they are in your care, and for any incident that leads to their loss, unless it was:

  • a genuine accident that was not caused by your carelessness or by breaking the rules; or
  • a robbery.
Accident or robbery must be reported. Tell dispatch immediately, and report it at the nearest police station (call 10111 if you are in danger). Send dispatch the police case number within 24 hours. If you do not report it, you are liable as if it were your fault.

Your safety comes first. Do not resist a robbery. Goods can be replaced; you cannot.

4. Breakage and loss are paid for by the driver, like breakage at a restaurant

If you are liable under section 3, you pay for what was broken or lost, the same way a waiter pays for a glass they drop. This is how it works:

  • Only the real loss. You pay what it costs us to replace or refund the affected items, and never more. There are no fines or extra charges.
  • You are told first and you can explain. We check what happened (time stamps, location trail, the outlet's packing record). We tell you in writing, on WhatsApp is fine, what happened and the amount. You then have 48 hours to tell us why you should not pay. We consider what you say and tell you our decision.
  • No deduction without that. We never take money from you for a loss before you have had the chance to explain.
  • How it is paid. You can pay it back in cash or by EFT. If you do not, we may deduct it from the payouts we owe you. By signing below you agree to this in advance, in writing.
  • Never more than a quarter at a time. On any one payday we deduct at most 25% of what is due to you on that payday. Whatever is left carries over to the next payouts until it is paid.
  • Genuine accident or robbery, reported properly: you pay nothing (section 3).
  • If you stop driving with us, what you still owe stays payable, but only as far as the law allows. You keep all your rights under the law.

5. Areas we do not deliver to

We decide which areas are no-delivery areas, and we may change them at any time. Do not accept or deliver to an address in one. If you are unsure, ask dispatch first. You may also decline any address that is unsafe.

6. Alcohol rules

Check ID (18+) at the door. No ID, under 18 or drunk means no handover. Never hand over without the customer's delivery PIN. Never leave alcohol unattended.

7. Driver agreement (sign once)

Print this page, sign it and give it to dispatch. We keep a copy in your staff file.
