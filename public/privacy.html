Privacy Policy

Version 2.0 · Last updated: 20 November 2026 · Replaces the version of 11 November 2026
The short version (the full policy below is what counts):
  • We collect what we need to take your order, deliver it safely and to the right adult, take payment and keep our records.
  • We never sell your personal information.
  • Marketing messages only go to people who opt in. Order updates are not marketing.
  • The partner outlet that packs your order does not get your name, phone number or exact address. The driver does, because the driver needs them to find you.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or complain to the Information Regulator. See sections 11 and 12.

5. Who we share it with

5.1 We share only what is needed. We share information with:

  • The partner outlet that packs your order: the items, the general area, the order code and the driver's name. Not your name, phone number or exact address.
  • The driver who delivers your order (only once they have taken the job): your name, phone number, address, landmark, pin and the items and cash total, so that they can find you and hand over to the right adult. Your delivery PIN stays with you.
  • Our staff: our dispatch and support team, who see orders so that they can run deliveries and help you.
  • Yoco (card payments) and your bank.
  • Our service providers (operators) who host and run the Platform: Supabase (sign-in and database), Render (servers), Resend (email), Google (address suggestions and maps), Cloudflare (visit statistics, if switched on), and WhatsApp, if you message us there. They may only use the information to do their job for us.
  • Professional advisers such as accountants, auditors and lawyers.
  • Authorities: the police, the Eastern Cape Liquor Board, SARS, courts and regulators, where the law requires or allows it, or to stop a crime or protect someone's safety.
  • A buyer or successor if we sell or restructure the business, on terms that protect your information.

5.2 We do not sell your personal information. We do not let advertisers target you using it.

5.3 Where we use an operator, we require it to keep your information secure and confidential and to follow POPIA.

6. Sending information outside South Africa

6.1 Our servers and database are in Frankfurt, Germany. Some other providers (for example email, maps and analytics) may handle information in Europe, the United States or elsewhere.

6.2 We send information abroad only where POPIA section 72 allows it: the country or provider is bound by laws or an agreement that give protection that is substantially similar to POPIA, or it is needed to carry out your order, or you consent. By placing an order or using the Platform you agree that your information may be handled in this way.

7. Marketing

7.1 We send marketing (specials, new arrivals) by email, WhatsApp or SMS only if you opted in. The box is never ticked for you. You can opt in or out at sign-up, at checkout or in your profile.

7.2 Every marketing message says who it is from and has a simple way to stop it, including an unsubscribe link. When you opt out we keep a record of that so that we do not message you again.

7.3 Order updates, delivery messages, security notices and replies to your questions are service messages. We send them whether or not you opted in to marketing.

8. Location

8.1 If you tap "use my location" at checkout, your phone shares a pin with us once so that the driver can find you. You can type your address and a landmark instead.

8.2 While your order is out for delivery, the driver's live location is shown on your tracking page. We keep only the driver's latest position on the order, and it is not shown after delivery or cancellation.

8.3 To prove a delivery, the driver's phone confirms its location when the PIN is entered.

9. Storage on your device

9.1 We do not use advertising cookies or trackers.

9.2 The Platform stores small items on your phone or browser so that it works: your sign-in session; your cart; your address and contact details so that you do not type them again; your favourites; that you passed the age notice; and whether you dismissed the install or welcome prompts. You can clear these in your browser or phone settings. If you do, you may be signed out and lose your cart.

9.3 The app also keeps pages and images on your phone (a cache) so that it opens quickly.

9.4 We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, when it is switched on, to count visits. It does not use cookies and does not identify you.

11. Your rights

11.1 You may ask us to:

  • confirm whether we hold your personal information, and give you a copy, and tell you who has had access to it;
  • correct information that is wrong, out of date or incomplete;
  • delete information we no longer need or are not allowed to keep;
  • stop using your information for a purpose, or for direct marketing, and withdraw consent you gave (this does not affect what we already did while your consent was in place);
  • tell you about a decision based on automated processing (section 13); and
  • tell you about a security breach that affects you.

11.2 Some requests have limits. We may have to keep a record to meet the law, protect a legal claim or finish an order. If we cannot do what you ask we will explain why.

12. How to use your rights and complain

12.1 Email support@ekoyini.co.za with the subject line "Privacy", or WhatsApp 064 004 5465. We will ask you to prove who you are before we give out or change anything, so that nobody else can get your information. We aim to respond within 30 days. We do not charge you unless the law allows a fee, and we would tell you first.

12.2 You may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za, POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za, enquiries@inforegulator.org.za, 010 023 5200, JD House, 27 Stiemens Street, Braamfontein, Johannesburg, 2001. We would like the chance to sort it out first, but you do not have to wait.

13. Automated decisions

13.1 Whether you can pay cash on delivery is decided by rules in our system, based on the number of orders delivered to your account, the order amount and some safety checks. Unusual cash orders may be flagged for a staff member to look at. These rules do not stop you ordering: you can always pay by card or EFT. If you think a decision is wrong, email us and a person will look at it.

14. Security

14.1 We protect your information with measures that include: encrypted connections; locked-down database access with no direct public access; protected storage of passwords and PINs; staff access limited by role and logged; limits on repeated and automated requests; and checks that payment confirmations really come from our payment provider.

14.2 No system is perfectly secure. Please keep your password, PIN and phone safe, and tell us if you suspect misuse. If a breach puts your information at risk, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

15. Drivers and partner outlet staff

15.1 If you use our driver or outlet app we also handle your phone number, a protected sign-in PIN, the devices you use, your delivery and order activity, your live location while you are working a job, and records we need to pay you. We use these to run deliveries and orders, keep customers safe, pay you and meet the law.

15.2 Your location is shared with the customer only while their order is out for delivery. If you use phone alerts, we keep a record of your device so that we can send them to it.

15.3 Your rights in section 11 apply to you too.

16. Children

16.1 The Platform is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we will delete it. If you think a child has used the Platform, please tell us.

17. Links to other sites

17.1 Other websites and apps we link to have their own privacy rules. We are not responsible for them.
