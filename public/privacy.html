1. Responsible party

[REGISTERED COMPANY NAME] (registration number [REG NO]), [PHYSICAL ADDRESS], is the responsible party under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). Our Information Officer is [NAME], reachable at [PRIVACY EMAIL].

2. What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number.
  • Delivery details: address, landmark notes and, while an order is active, the location you share for delivery.
  • Order history, payment method chosen and payment status. Card numbers are processed by Yoco and are not stored by us.
  • Age confirmation and ID check outcome at delivery (we do not keep copies of your ID).
  • Basic technical data such as device and browser type, used for security and to prevent abuse.

3. Why we use it

  • To process, deliver and support your orders (contract).
  • To verify legal drinking age and meet liquor law obligations (legal obligation).
  • To prevent fraud and keep the service secure (legitimate interest).
  • To send order updates by email, SMS or WhatsApp. Marketing messages are only sent with your consent and you can opt out at any time.

4. Who we share it with

Only what is needed, with: the partner outlet fulfilling your order, the driver delivering it (name, phone, address), our payment provider (Yoco), and our hosting, database, email and messaging providers. Some providers may store data outside South Africa; we only use providers with adequate protection as required by section 72 of POPIA. We never sell your personal information.

5. Driver location

While your order is out for delivery, the driver's live location is shown on your tracking page. It is not shown once the order is delivered or cancelled.

6. How long we keep it

Order and payment records are kept for [5] years for tax and accounting purposes. Other information is deleted or anonymised when no longer needed, or when you close your account, unless the law requires us to keep it.

7. Security

We use encrypted connections, access controls for staff, and limits on repeated requests to protect your information. If a breach affects you, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

8. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, object to its use, or withdraw consent for marketing. Email [PRIVACY EMAIL]. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za.

9. Children

Ekoyini is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

10. Changes

We will post any updates on this page with a new date. See also our Terms & Conditions.
