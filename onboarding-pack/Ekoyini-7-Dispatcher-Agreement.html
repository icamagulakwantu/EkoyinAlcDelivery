1. Position and start

1.1 The Dispatcher is employed part-time as Dispatcher, starting on ____ / ____ / ________, working remotely from a place of their choice with reliable signal.

1.2 The first 3 months are a probation period, with feedback at least once a month.

2. Duties

2.1 The Dispatcher carries out the duties in the Ekoyini Dispatcher Operations Manual (which may be updated, with notice), including watching orders, confirming EFT payments only when the money has arrived, assigning taverns and drivers, sending WhatsApp messages, acting on delay alerts and writing a handover note at the end of each shift.

2.2 The Dispatcher reports to the super admin of Ekoyini.

3. Hours

3.1 The Dispatcher works the shifts on the roster agreed each week (for example Friday and Saturday 16:00 to 24:00 and Sunday 12:00 to 20:00). Each shift has a 30-minute break when orders allow. Ordinary hours never exceed 45 a week.

3.2 Shift changes are agreed at least 48 hours ahead, except in an emergency.

4. Pay

4.1 R40 per hour worked on the roster (never less than the national minimum wage, R30.23 an hour from 1 March 2026), plus an order bonus of R5 for each order the Dispatcher handled during their shift that is delivered.

4.2 Paid by EFT every ______ (weekly / month-end), with a payslip showing hours, bonus and deductions.

4.3 Deductions: UIF (1%) and PAYE income tax if applicable. No other deductions without the Dispatcher's written consent.

5. Leave

5.1 Annual leave of 1 day for every 17 days worked; sick leave and family responsibility leave as in the Basic Conditions of Employment Act.

6. Equipment and access

6.1 The Dispatcher uses their own smartphone. The Employer pays a data allowance of R______ a month and gives access to the admin page and the business WhatsApp.

6.2 The admin login is personal and every action is recorded. The Dispatcher never shares it and signs out on shared devices.

7. Confidentiality (POPIA)

7.1 Customer, driver and tavern information, PINs, prices, payouts and business information are confidential. The Dispatcher uses them only to do the job, never shares them outside the admin page or with anyone not entitled to them, never screenshots or keeps them, and reports any leak at once. This continues after employment ends.

8. Conduct

8.1 No alcohol or drugs during shifts. Respectful to every customer, tavern and driver. Never confirms an EFT payment that has not arrived, never shares a PIN, never approves a delivery to a minor.

9. Ending

9.1 Notice: 1 week during the first 6 months, 2 weeks after that, in writing (WhatsApp is accepted). Dismissal follows a fair reason and a fair procedure under the Labour Relations Act.

9.2 On the last day the Employer removes the admin access and the Dispatcher returns or deletes any business information.
